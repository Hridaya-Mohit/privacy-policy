Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe  ·  Last updated: September 2025

Overview

Tic Tac Toe ("the App") is developed by GameZone. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

  • Phone number — collected when you sign in for multiplayer. Used solely to identify your account and let friends find you.
  • Display name — the name you choose in settings. Visible to friends and opponents.
  • Game data — match history, win/loss/draw counts, and online status. Stored to power career stats and the friends system.
  • Device token (FCM) — collected to send push notifications for game invites and friend requests. Never shared with third parties.
  • Device identifier — used only to enforce single-device login (ensure your account is active on one device at a time).

How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To enable real-time multiplayer gameplay
  • To let friends find and invite you by phone number
  • To send push notifications for game invites and friend requests
  • To display career stats on the match result screen

Advertising

The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation).


For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:


  • Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) — Google
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Games Services — achievements and leaderboards

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) and retained as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
